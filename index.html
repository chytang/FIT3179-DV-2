Who is watching?

Compare bird sightings across Australian states by total sightings recorded and sightings per person. Switch between the maps to explore reporting patterns. Hover over a state to explore its data.

Higher sighting totals may reflect more birdwatchers or greater reporting activity, rather than greater bird abundance.

Where do sightings cluster?

Each circle sits on a one-degree square of the map, roughly 110 km from north to south. Larger circles indicate more sightings per grid square. Use the dropdown under the map to select a bird and explore where its sightings cluster. The numbers 1 to 3 mark the three busiest squares.
